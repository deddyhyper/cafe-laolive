## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an earthy, sun-drenched Mediterranean hospitality experience tailored for an artisanal coffee house and culinary eatery. The aesthetic balances pastoral warmth with refined modern dining elegance, evoking visual memories of terracotta pottery, sun-bleached stone, and olive groves under afternoon light.

The target audience comprises discerning culinary enthusiasts, remote professionals seeking inspiring cafe environments, and patrons valuing thoughtful gastronomy, artisanal coffee, and slow living. The interface evokes unhurried calm, sensual warmth, approachable luxury, and organic craftsmanship.

Drawing from an intersection of **Warm Editorial Minimalism** and **Tactile Organic Warmth**, the visual language prioritizes generous negative space, editorial-grade typography, softly rounded organic containers, and subtle warm-tinted depth rather than synthetic digital effects.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in sun-cured Mediterranean earth tones and botanical olive shades.

- **Primary (`#4A5D23` with deep shades `#2F3E1E` and mid `#556B2F`)**: Represents botanical olive foliage. Deployed for primary actions, critical brand touchpoints, active states, and refined emblems.
- **Secondary (`#C86D51` with deep terracotta `#A34E36`)**: Clay and baked terracotta. Used strategically for culinary highlights, call-out badges, secondary CTAs, seasonal menus, and warm notifications.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` with deep amber `#B8860B`)**: Warm olive oil, golden crema, and raw timber. Applied to star ratings, artisanal accents, dividers, and decorative badges.
- **Neutral (`#231F20` with ground charcoal `#1C1917`)**: Rich roasted espresso. Used for high-contrast typography, structural outlines, and crisp footer surfaces.
- **Canvas & Surface Tier (`#FDFBF7`, `#F4EFE6`, `#EAE2D5`)**: Sun-bleached linen and warm oat milk. Replaces stark whites entirely to ensure eye comfort and organic warmth across card surfaces, modals, and canvas backgrounds.

## Typography

The typography pairing reconciles editorial romance with effortless digital utility.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Expresses traditional European bistro menus, culinary literature, and timeless elegance. Generous line heights preserve rhythmic grace. Italicized variants can be employed for French culinary terms, origin notes, and seasonal highlights.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides crisp geometric clarity with subtle rounded terminals that echo the warm, approachable character of the identity. Used for ingredient specifications, menu descriptions, pricing tables, operational controls, and administrative interactions.
- Mobile scaling steps down display and primary headline sizes to retain balanced margins without harsh multiline wrapping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an open Mediterranean courtyard—airy, naturally structured, and unhurried. 

- **Grid Architecture**: Desktop interfaces employ a 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded by a max container width of 1280px. Tablet breakpoints shift to an 8-column grid (`gutter: 1.25rem`), and mobile screens collapse to a 4-column flow with fluid edge margins (`1.25rem`).
- **Spatial Rhythm**: Layout distances rely on an 8pt base scale. Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` and higher multiples) separates distinct culinary chapters (e.g., Pour Over Profiles, Brunch Plates, Table Reservations) to maintain an editorial browsing pace rather than a crowded retail experience.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids sterile drop shadows and icy glassmorphism, relying instead on **Tonal Clay Surfaces** and **Warm Ambient Shadows**.

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas begins at `#FDFBF7`. Resting cards sit atop `#F4EFE6`. Interactive floats, modals, and dropdown overlays use crisp `#FFFFFF` or elevated `#FDFBF7` bounded by whisper-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(74, 93, 35, 0.12)`).
- **Warm Shadow Character**: Shadows are diffused and tinted with raw umber and espresso pigments rather than cold black.
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(74, 93, 35, 0.04)`
  - *Interactive Hover Elevation*: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(200, 109, 81, 0.06)`
  - *Modal & Overlay Elevation*: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.16)`
- **Atmospheric Dividers**: Subtle hairline borders tinted with warm beige (`#EAE2D5`) separate sections without creating visual grid locks.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape profile (`level 2`), referencing smooth river stones, ceramic cups, terracotta tiles, and olive leaves.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem / 8px`)**: Applied to standard interactive form inputs, buttons, filter pills, and small thumbnail containers.
- **Large Radius (`1rem / 16px`)**: Applied to menu cards, table booking panels, image galleries, and notification toasts.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`1.5rem / 24px`)**: Reserved for primary modal sheets, featured promotional banners, and signature dining cards.
- **Organic Accent Radii**: Selected primary hero containers may pair diagonal rounded corners (e.g., top-left and bottom-right at 2rem) to subtly evoke classical architectural arches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Olive (`#4A5D23`) background, `#FDFBF7` text, subtle 0.5rem radius, padded with `0.75rem 1.75rem`. On hover, transitions to `#2F3E1E` with a slight warm shadow lift.
- **Secondary**: Terracotta (`#C86D51`) background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for immediate ordering or table reservation prompts. Hover state shifts to `#A34E36`.
- **Outline / Ghost**: 1px border in `#4A5D23`, transparent background, Olive text. On hover, fills with warm cream overlay (`#F4EFE6`).

### Cards & Menu Containers
- Surface tone of `#F4EFE6` or `#FDFBF7` with a 1px border in `#EAE2D5`. Corner radius of 1rem (`rounded-lg`).
- Product imagery within cards features organic soft-radiused clipping.
- Menu listing cards incorporate an editorial price line item, connecting dish titles in `Playfair Display` to prices via delicate dotted leaders or balanced whitespace.

### Chips & Badges
- **Dietary & Origin Chips (e.g., "Single Origin", "Vegan", "Estate Harvest")**: Background of `#EAE2D5` with dark espresso text (`#231F20`), or light terracotta tint (`rgba(200, 109, 81, 0.12)`) with `#A34E36` text. Fully rounded pill profile with `label-sm` tracking.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Background set to `#FFFFFF` against canvas `#FDFBF7`. Subtle border in `#EAE2D5` with `0.5rem` radius.
- On focus, input activates a 1.5px solid ring in `#4A5D23` with a soft warm-tinted halo (`rgba(74, 93, 35, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squircle checkboxes (`4px` radius) and circular radios with a `1.5px` border in `#4A5D23`. When checked, filled with Olive (`#4A5D23`) and featuring a cream `#FDFBF7` checkmark.

### Specialty Eatery Components
- **Origin & Tasting Note Palette**: Compact multi-tone chips indicating roast level, acidity, process (washed/natural), and altitude.
- **Table Reservation Bar**: Streamlined sticky horizontal pill housing date, party size, and seating area (Courtyard / Espresso Bar / Dining Room) selectors.
